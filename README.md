# The Shattered Crown

> *Whoever reunites the Crown reshapes the world The question is whether you should*

## About
**The Shattered Crown** is a fantasy visual novel made with Ren'Py
Once a crown held five kingdoms together Then a king grew greedy A queen grew afraid A general grew ambitious
That was five years ago The kingdoms have been bleeding ever since
You've been walking for three days You're tired You're hungry
And something in your pack has been glowing since dawn

## Story

A chance encounter in a dying village pulls you into something much larger than a missing elder and a haunted forest Three shards of a Shattered Crown A world that needs saving  or doesn't Companions with debts of their own And at the end of it all a choice that no one can make for you
What you do with the Crown defines everything

## Characters
**You**  A traveller with no name until you give one Your courage cunning and mercy shape the world around you
**Lyra**  Village scout Sharp loyal and quietly carrying something she hasn't said out loud yet
**Kael**  Former Ironguard knight Living alone in a haunted forest for reasons he's not ready to explain
**Sera**  Owes a debt to someone dangerous Knows more about magic than she's letting on
**Tobias**  Visionary engineer His inventions are impractical until suddenly they aren't
**Elder Maren**  Stubborn as stone and twice as hard Knows more about the Crown than anyone
**Vex**  She wants the shard Her reasons are more complicated than they appear

## Endings
There are **3 main endings** to discover  plus a secret that changes how you see all of them

 ✦ The Crown Restored *(Hero's Ending)*
 ✦ The Crown Claimed *(Shadow Ending)*
 ✦ The Crown Destroyed *(Exile's Ending)*
 ◌ ???
The game tracks your playthroughs The road remembers you

## Features
 Branching dialogue with persistent choices across runs
 Three stat tracks  courage cunning mercy  that shape how characters respond
 Four companion side quests
 Named protagonist (you choose)
 New Game+ acknowledgement of your previous endings
